================================================================================
Skill Engineering ERP - Complete Project Data Pipeline & User Role Guide
================================================================================

Purpose:
This guide details the end-to-end work pipeline of the Skill Engineering ERP system, 
tracing every transaction from initial customer lead creation to project closeout.
It lists the precise roles responsible for each stage, what inputs are required in 
each section, the status transitions, and the database tables affected.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Role Directory & Login Info
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Use the "/login" page to start a demo session for testing. You can login as:
- Super Admin: Full read/write access across all modules.
- Marketing Executive / Manager: Manages CRM, leads, requirements, and quotation approvals.
- QS Engineer: Manages estimations, lines, and BOQ builders.
- Project Manager (PM) / Technical Officer (TO): Manages projects, milestones, progress updates, stock requests, and petty cash.
- Stock Manager / Store Keeper: Manages PO approvals, inventory, and goods receipt notes (GRNs).
- HR Manager: Manages departments, positions, employee assignments, and payroll.
- Vehicle Manager: Manages vehicle specs, meter logs, fuel logs, and maintenance logs.
- Accountant / Finance Manager: Manages cash book, general ledger, petty cash settlements, client payments, and journal entries.

--------------------------------================================================
2. The Work Pipeline: Step-by-Step Flow
--------------------------------================================================

STAGE 1: LEAD CREATION & CUSTOMER INTAKE
----------------------------------------
- Role: Marketing Executive
- Action: Register a prospect customer and record their initial project request.
- Status Path: lead status initialized as "new"
- What to Fill:
  - Customer Section: Customer Code (e.g. CUS-001), Company Name, Contact Person, Phone, Email, Location (Colombo, Galle, etc.).
  - Lead Section: Lead Code (e.g. LEAD-1001), Project Title, Project Type (e.g. Drawing only, 2D/3D, Construction only, Full Project), Estimated Budget (LKR), Requirement Description.
- Tables Covered: `customers`, `leads`, `lead_status_logs`

STAGE 2: REQUIREMENT CAPTURE & WORKFLOW ASSIGNMENT
--------------------------------------------------
- Role: Marketing Executive or Marketing Manager
- Action: Detail drawing/construction requirements, set the workflow type, and assign teams.
- Status Path: lead status "new" -> "under_review"
- What to Fill:
  - Drawing Requirements: As-built plans needed, 2D layout, 3D renderings required.
  - Construction Requirements: Site details, night shifts preferred, structural safety parameters.
  - Workflow & Team Selection: Choose workflow (e.g. FULL_PROJECT) and assign 2D Team, 3D Team, or QS Team.
- Tables Covered: `leads`, `lead_communications`, `file_attachments`

STAGE 3: QS ESTIMATION & BOQ BUILDING
-------------------------------------
- Role: QS Engineer
- Action: Prepare cost estimates (material, labour, equipment, overheads) and build a detailed BOQ.
- Status Path: estimation status "draft" -> "in_progress" -> "ready_for_quotation"
- What to Fill:
  - Cost Summary Section: Material Cost Total, Labour Cost Total, Equipment Cost Total, Overhead Cost Total, Profit Margin Percent (e.g., 15%).
  - Estimation Lines: Category (e.g., Civil, Mechanical), Description, Quantity, Unit, Unit Rate, Remarks.
  - BOQ Builder Panel: Group/Section Title, Item Name, Item Description, Quantity, Unit, Unit Price.
- Tables Covered: `estimations`, `estimation_lines`, `boqs`, `boq_sections`, `boq_items`

STAGE 4: QUOTATION GENERATION & MARK READY
------------------------------------------
- Role: QS Engineer
- Action: Clicking "Mark Ready for Quotation" in the estimation page submits the estimate, automatically generates a quotation, and updates the lead status.
- Status Path:
  - Estimation: "ready_for_quotation"
  - Quotation: created in "draft" status
  - Lead: transitions from "under_review" -> "qs_estimation_pending"
- What to Fill:
  - Triggered automatically by the system. Links the estimation, BOQ ID, and calculates commercial totals (Subtotal, Tax/Profit Margin Value, Grand Total).
- Tables Covered: `quotations`, `boqs` (updated with `quotation_id`)

STAGE 5: COMMERCIAL REVIEW & CLIENT SUBMISSION
----------------------------------------------
- Role: Marketing Manager
- Action: Review the quotation, verify payment terms, and send the proposal to the client.
- Status Path:
  - Quotation: "draft" -> "client_sent"
  - Lead: "qs_estimation_pending" -> "quotation_submitted"
- What to Fill:
  - Payment Terms Text: e.g. "40% advance, 50% progress, 10% handover".
  - Valid Until Date: date limit for quotation validity.
  - Notes & Exclusions: Specific items excluded from the quote.
- Tables Covered: `quotations` (updated with `sent_at`), `leads`, `audit_logs`

STAGE 6: CLIENT APPROVAL (CLIENT PORTAL OR MANUAL)
--------------------------------------------------
- Role: Client (via Portal) or Marketing Manager (on behalf of client)
- Action: Client opens the portal using their token, reviews the BOQ, and submits a decision.
- Status Path:
  - Quotation: "client_sent" -> "approved" (or "rejected" / "revision_requested")
  - Lead: "quotation_submitted" -> "approved" (or "rejected" / "revision_requested")
- What to Fill:
  - Client Response Panel: Decision (Approved / Rejected / Revision Requested), Remarks or reasons for rejection/revision.
- Tables Covered: `client_responses`, `quotations`, `leads`

STAGE 7: PROJECT CREATION & SITE GENERATION
-------------------------------------------
- Role: Marketing Manager or Super Admin
- Action: Convert the approved quotation into an active project.
- Status Path:
  - Project: "created" -> "assigned" -> "in_progress"
- What to Fill:
  - Project Details: Project Manager, TO, Start Date, End Date.
  - System automatically creates:
    1. A site record linked to the project (e.g. STE-9874).
    2. A client access token (`client_access_tokens` table) to let the client log into `/client/[token]` and view real-time updates.
- Tables Covered: `projects`, `sites`, `client_access_tokens`, `project_status_logs`

STAGE 8: SITE WORKFORCE & HR MANAGEMENT
---------------------------------------
- Role: HR Manager
- Action: Assign employees to sites, log attendance, and run payroll.
- What to Fill:
  - Employee Form: Code (EMP-001), Name, Department (Projects, QS, HR, Finance), Designation, Salary Type (monthly/daily), Salary Amount.
  - Attendance: Date, Attendance Status (present, absent, leave), Location/Site assignment.
  - Payroll Form: Batch Code, Period start/end dates, approve payroll batched lines.
- Tables Covered: `departments`, `positions`, `employees`, `employee_site_assignments`, `attendance`, `payroll_batches`, `payroll_lines`

STAGE 9: VEHICLE & LOGISTICS ASSIGNMENT
---------------------------------------
- Role: Vehicle Manager or TO
- Action: Assign vehicles to the project and track mileage and fuel usage.
- What to Fill:
  - Vehicle Form: Vehicle Code, License No, Category, Ownership, Status.
  - Assignment: Project ID, Employee ID, Assigned Date.
  - Meter Log: Current Odometer reading, Date.
  - Fuel Log: Liters filled, Cost (LKR), Receipt Attachments.
- Tables Covered: `vehicles`, `vehicle_assignments`, `vehicle_meter_logs`, `vehicle_fuel_logs`, `vehicle_maintenance_logs`

STAGE 10: MATERIAL REQUISITION & STOCK MANAGEMENT
-------------------------------------------------
- Role: PM (to request), Stock Manager (to approve PO), Store Keeper (to receive)
- Action: PM requests materials -> Stock Manager creates PO -> Store Keeper receives and updates inventory.
- Status Path:
  - Stock Request: "draft" -> "submitted" -> "approved" -> "converted_to_po"
  - Purchase Order: "draft" -> "pending_approval" -> "approved" -> "issued"
- What to Fill:
  - Stock Request: Project, Site, Required Date, Material name, Quantity needed, Unit of Measure.
  - Purchase Order: Supplier, Linked Stock Request ID, Quantity, Unit Price.
  - Goods Receipt Note (GRN): Received Date, Quantity Received, Quantity Damaged.
- Tables Covered: `stock_requests`, `stock_request_items`, `purchase_orders`, `purchase_order_items`, `goods_receipts`, `goods_receipt_items`, `site_inventory`, `stock_movements`

STAGE 11: PROJECT EXPENSES & PETTY CASH ISSUANCE
------------------------------------------------
- Role: Project Manager (to record expense / request cash), Accountant (to post ledger)
- Action: PM records project expenditures and requests petty cash. Accountant approves and logs transactions in the Chart of Accounts.
- What to Fill:
  - Expense Form: Category (material, labour, transport, misc), Date, Vendor, Amount, Payment Method, Notes.
  - Petty Cash Form: Employee, Allocated Amount, Issued Date, Settled Amount.
- Tables Covered: `project_expenses`, `petty_cash`

STAGE 12: FINANCIAL JOURNAL ENTRY & DOUBLE-ENTRY LEDGER
-------------------------------------------------------
- Role: Accountant or Finance Manager
- Action: Auto-triggered by project expense, payment, or petty cash. Performs ledger bookkeeping.
- Status Path: "draft" -> "posted"
- What to Fill:
  - Journal Entry Details: Reference No, Transaction Date, Project ID.
  - Journal Lines: Dr Expense/Asset Account, Cr Cash/Bank Account (ensures Sum of Debits == Sum of Credits).
- Tables Covered: `accounts`, `bank_accounts`, `journal_entries`, `journal_lines`, `client_payments`

STAGE 13: SITE PROGRESS UPDATES & CLIENT PORTAL UPDATES
-------------------------------------------------------
- Role: Technical Officer (TO) or Project Manager
- Action: Submit weekly progress summaries and photos to be shown to the client.
- What to Fill:
  - Progress Form: Update Date, Progress Title, Work Summary, Percent Complete, Client Visible (Check/Uncheck).
  - Documents Upload: Upload drawings, certificate scans, or milestone photos.
- Tables Covered: `site_progress_updates`, `file_attachments`

STAGE 14: PROJECT COMPLETION & CLOSEOUT
---------------------------------------
- Role: PM (to mark completed), Super Admin / Accountant (to close)
- Action: Close out project once work is complete, files are uploaded, and financials are settled.
- Status Path: Project "in_progress" -> "completed" -> "closed"
- What to Fill:
  - PM completes progress to 100%.
  - Validation: System prevents closing the project if there are pending PO receipts, unreconciled petty cash, or unpaid expenses.
- Tables Covered: `projects`, `project_status_logs`, `audit_logs`

--------------------------------================================================
3. Database Table Coverage Summary
--------------------------------================================================
The end-to-end project workflow covers the following Supabase tables:
- CRM: `customers`, `leads`, `lead_communications`, `lead_status_logs`
- QS: `estimations`, `estimation_lines`, `boqs`, `boq_sections`, `boq_items`
- Sales: `quotations`, `client_responses`, `client_access_tokens`
- Project: `projects`, `sites`, `project_status_logs`, `project_activity_logs`, `site_progress_updates`
- HR: `departments`, `positions`, `employees`, `employee_site_assignments`, `attendance`, `payroll_batches`, `payroll_lines`
- Stock: `suppliers`, `materials`, `units_of_measure`, `stock_requests`, `stock_request_items`, `purchase_orders`, `purchase_order_items`, `goods_receipts`, `goods_receipt_items`, `site_inventory`, `stock_movements`
- Vehicle: `vehicles`, `vehicle_assignments`, `vehicle_meter_logs`, `vehicle_fuel_logs`, `vehicle_maintenance_logs`
- Finance: `accounts`, `bank_accounts`, `project_expenses`, `petty_cash`, `client_payments`, `journal_entries`, `journal_lines`
- Auditing: `audit_logs`, `file_attachments`
